Vector style, animation method and loop structure

Art direction

  • Flat vector, thick rounded outlines in navy, two-tone fills from the Devdha palette (navy, blue, light blue, cream, gold) plus coral and green accents.
  • Faceless characters (dot-free faces, simple hair shapes) so they work in any culture and are quick to animate.
  • Every vignette 3.8 s: 1 s in, 2 s action, 0.8 s arrow; the product text types under it in EN/HI/MR.
  • All animation is built in SVG and driven by time, so it renders frame-by-frame like the earlier videos (looped steam, swinging tags, scrolling straps, layered 3D print).
  • Edge arrows and the gold edge bar are part of the frame so they line up with the physical shelf.

Rate-free

No price on any vignette. Text only says what it is and invites people in.
