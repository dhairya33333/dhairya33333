Style rules, QA and decisions

Motion and layout rules

  • Glow words: Poppins 600–700, white with a blue halo; the key word of a phrase in gold.
  • Enter: blur 18 px → sharp, scale .92 → 1, 0.35 s. Exit reversed, 0.3 s.
  • Swipes are horizontal with motion blur; never a hard cut.
  • UI mocks type, tap and fill on screen; no still screenshots.
  • Hindi/Marathi: Noto Sans Devanagari, same sizes; word order follows the translation.
  • Keep all text in the central 90 %; readable from the street at 3 m.
  • Silent for the shop; optional sound version for Instagram.

QA before it goes live

  • No price or "from ₹" visible anywhere (automated text scan).
  • Every client tile has permission or is removed.
  • Hindi and Marathi read by a fluent speaker.
  • QR scans from 3 m; daytime glare test behind glass; loop seam check.
