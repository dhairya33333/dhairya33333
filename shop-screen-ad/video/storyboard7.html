The Devdha vector kit: what keeps it consistent

style rules and motion presets

Look

  • No outlines. Chunky, rounded flat shapes; one big circle behind every story.
  • Palette: navy, Devdha blue, olive, mustard, terracotta, cream, warm skin tones, coral. Every story uses 4–5 of these.
  • Characters: friendly faces with eyebrows, rosy cheeks, a smile, sometimes a wink; hands with finger lines; texture strokes on clothes.
  • Diverse cast: different skin tones, hair and ages (adults, team, child).
  • Details: steam wisps, sparkles, hearts, hatch marks.
  • One shared parts library (bust, face, hands, mug, hair styles) so new products stay on-style.

Motion presets (same on every story)

PresetWhat it does
pop-incircle grows from 0; character/product scales .85 → 1 with a small overshoot, 0.4 s
breathe±4 px body float, 2.4 s loop
blink / winkblink every 3 s; wink once on the beat
steamwisp waves upward on a 1.4 s loop
swingpendulum ±8° for tags, badges, frames
twinklesparkles scale and fade, offset phases
draw-onlines and paths draw in (layers, strings, charts)
pointarrow bounces 3× at the zone edge; edge glow pulses gold
exitcircle shrinks and slides toward the arrow direction (up, right or down)
Timing per story: 0.5 s in · 2.2 s action · 0.8 s point · 0.5 s out = 4 s (3D print stories 5 s).
