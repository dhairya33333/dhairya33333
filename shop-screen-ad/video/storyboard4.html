Style rules and decisions

Motion rules

  • Glow words: Poppins 600–700, white with a soft blue halo; the last word of a phrase glows gold.
  • Enter: blur 18 px → sharp with a 0.92 → 1 scale in 0.35 s; exit the reverse in 0.3 s.
  • Swipes use horizontal slide with motion blur, never a hard cut.
  • UI mocks type and tap on screen; no static screenshots.
  • Hindi and Marathi: Noto Sans Devanagari at the same sizes; word-by-word follows the translation word order.
  • Landscape layout: words left, working UI right, or full-width cutout swipe. Keep all text inside the central 90 %.
  • Seamless loop: S13 hands over to S1 of the next language with a blur-wipe.
