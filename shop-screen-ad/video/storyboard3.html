Our Work wall · the idea

grid zoom-out, the signature moment
hero clip fills the screen (REEL.mp4)
1 · Hero fills the screen (6 s)
zoom-out 1: hero + 8 neighbours
2 · Pull back: 3×3, hero in the middle
zoom-out 2: the wall
3 · Wall of work, slow diagonal drift
zoom-out 3: massive grid; Devdha logo forms in the middle
4 · Massive grid; logo forms
  • One continuous camera move: a real scale animation from the hero tile (1×) out to the whole wall (about 0.08×); tiles keep their true layout, so the viewer sees the hero was always one tile of many.
  • Mixed tiles: posters and designs, product photos with the background removed on soft blue panels, 3D prints, and short looping video tiles that keep playing as the camera pulls back.
  • Motion rules: ease-in-out, no shake; after each zoom-out the wall drifts slowly for a beat so tiles can be read; a thin blue outline marks the hero before the next push-in.
  • Caption: only at the end of the zoom: "Our Work / हमारा काम / आमचे काम" and "Designed. Printed. Made in Dombivli." (हिन्दी: डिज़ाइन। प्रिंट। डोंबिवली में तैयार। · मराठी: डिझाइन. प्रिंट. डोंबिवलीत तयार.)
  • Removing backgrounds: done on products (keychains, mugs, 3D prints). Not possible on the white T-shirt: it stays a rectangular photo tile.
