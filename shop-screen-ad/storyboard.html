How the ad works

The goal of every loop

  1. Make a passer-by glance up (movement + bright logo).
  2. Tell them what the shop is in one line.
  3. Give a reason to walk in (a price, a service, an offer).
  4. Make it easy to act (QR, phone, address, hours).

Design rules (outdoor screen, 3–5 m away)

  • Headline ≥ 8% of screen height (≈ 90 px at 1080p); supporting text ≥ 5% (≈ 55 px). Nothing smaller than 40 px.
  • ≤ 8 words per scene. People give you 3–5 seconds, so each scene must make sense alone.
  • High contrast: white / bright text on deep navy. No thin fonts, no pale pastel text.
  • 5% safe margin on every side (TVs can crop the edge).
  • Silent: street noise wins. Nothing depends on sound.
  • The shop name + area appears in the first and last scene, so every loop re-introduces you.
  • Prices are "from ₹…" and match the website. No unprovable claims ("cheapest", "fastest").
  • QR code ≥ 15% of screen height, on screen ≥ 8 s, high contrast (dark on white).

Motion style. Calm, bold and readable: words rise in line by line, products slide or scale in, one move per scene. Transitions are quick wipes (0.6 s), so nothing flashes or spins on a busy street. The last frame is identical to the first, so the loop doesn't jump.
Check before publishing: every price shows "plus GST" on your website — decide whether the screen says "+ GST" in small print. Offers need an end date so the screen never advertises something expired.

How to capture photos & clips (phone is fine)

Product photos

  • Landscape, phone held level, as close as you can while the product fills 60–70% of the frame.
  • Plain background (a white sheet or table), daylight near a window, no flash, no filters.
  • Take 3 angles per item: front, slightly angled, close-up of the print.
  • Clean the item first; fingerprints and dust show on screen.

3D printer clip

  • 6–10 seconds, landscape, phone on a stack of books or tripod so it doesn't shake.
  • Show the nozzle moving, then a finished part held up. Good light, no people's faces unless they agree.

Shop & screen-location photos

  • One photo of the shop front, one of where the screen will hang, and one from 3–5 m away at normal eye height.
  • Tell me if the spot gets direct sun, and at what time.

How to send

  • Attach files in this chat, or give a shared Google Drive / WhatsApp-to-self link. Send originals, not screenshots.

Screen set-up checklist
  • Brightness at maximum; screen saver and sleep off.
  • Autoplay + loop on power-up (test by switching the power off and on).
  • Mount in shade or under an awning, angled slightly down to cut glare.
  • USB sticks: format FAT32 or exFAT; if FAT32, the file must be under 4 GB (ours will be far smaller).
What I won't do: invent offers, prices, client names, awards or customer numbers. Anything on screen comes from your catalogue or from you.
Optional extras
  • An upright (1080 × 1920) version of the same loop.
  • A 15-second short loop for slow evenings.
  • A monthly refresh: send me the new offer and I re-export.
