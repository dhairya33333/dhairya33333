The Spark

A small gold idea that jumps out of the screen and walks people to your shop
  • Original only. No client work, no Drive assets, no photos. Everything is vector art, type and animated screens drawn for this video.
  • About 75 s per language (English → हिन्दी → मराठी, 3 min 45 s in all). 16:9, silent, rate-free, seamless loop.
  • Real shelves: gold arrows point up, right and down at your real products.

  • The Spark: a rounded four-point star in gold with a lighter core, two dot eyes and a small mouth, and a soft glow. Tiny arms appear only when it waves, shrugs or gives a thumbs-up.
  • Theme: deep navy base, electric blue and mid-blue rooms, white type, gold only for the Spark, edge arrows and highlights. Coral appears once or twice as an accent.
  • Shapes: flat, chunky, rounded; no outlines; soft glows.
  • Type: Poppins; words appear one at a time with a blue glow; the key word is gold. Hindi and Marathi in Noto Sans Devanagari.
  • Motion presets: pop-in (overshoot), blink, hop, swing, morph, bulge, draw-on, point (arrow bounces ×3 with a gold edge glow), blur-swipe between scenes.
  • Logo: the two U-shapes slide together and lock; the Spark pops out of the mark at the end.
